# What "done" means here, and what is still measured as missing

Date: 11 October 2026. Written because the standing request is to test until everything runs
seamlessly **and** to read the documentation to know the quality that is required. The two places
that define it are `docs/plans/2026-10-08-pos-native-estacion.md` (the rubric, §Rúbrica and
§Finalización) and `docs/plans/2026-10-09-pos-estacion-complete.md` (§Auditoría de cobertura and
§Criterio de cierre). This note puts today's measurements against them, so the gap is a list and
not a feeling.

## The bar, in the words of the plan

| Where                 | Requirement                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Rubric                | Seven weighted criteria: task and hierarchy 20 %, visual quality 20 %, coverage 15 %, adaptation 15 %, accessibility 15 %, response and recovery 10 %, fluency 5 %.                                                                                                                          |
| Rubric, accessibility | "Objetivos de 48, contraste, foco, semántica, **texto aumentado** y selección."                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| §Finalización         | The client must cover the whole interface, compile, and pass its relevant tests. Native composition and operation must be confirmed. **No critical or important gap may remain in tasks, accessibility, or legibility.**                                                                     |
| §Finalización         | "La ausencia de prueba conserva la brecha" — an untested requirement stays open.                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| §Criterio de cierre   | The visual layer is integrated in every POS surface and keeps the existing commands and permissions. The art score is **8.7/10**, and integration does not turn it into 10.0. The empirical tasks listed in the coverage table stay pending; **no row is approved by the presence of code**. |

Two details in that bar were worth the owner's eye, and both have an answer now:

1. **The pointer floor is written as 48 in the rubric and was measured as 44** — and the product's
   own token is `UmiTouchTarget.minimum = 48.0`. `pos-device-inventory.mjs` documented a
   `--control-min` flag that did not exist, so every report so far could only state the 44 figure.
   The flag is implemented and the iMin walk was re-run at 48: **0 controls under 48 px, on all
   seven surfaces**, with nothing in the 44–48 band. The bar is met either way; the question of
   which number applies is now the owner's preference, not a gap.
2. **"Texto aumentado" was a real gap, and it is fixed in source.** At `Muy grande` the iMin's
   sign-in screen put `Continuar` below the fold (defect 2 of the device report). Reproduced at the
   till's real logical size (1396×737), it turned out to break at `Grande` too, and the fix plus
   two guard tests (sign-in and enrolment, at 100 %, 150 % and 200 %) are in this branch. The
   on-device proof waits on the APK rebuild.

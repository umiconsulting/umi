## Source notes

- Toast images were originally captured on 2026-10-12 from the official
  articles linked above; the articles still resolve on 2026-10-18.
- Fudo split frames were decoded from the provider's current public support GIF.
- Square community screenshots are not used as product evidence; the
  independent comments are linked directly in the report.
- Umi screenshots are from the installed staging host after testing. The
  staging floor plan contains four occupied test tables and two open accounts,
  so the screenshots show realistic populated states.

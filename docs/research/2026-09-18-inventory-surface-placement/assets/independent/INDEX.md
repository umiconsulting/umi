# Independent and third-party screenshots

- Captured: 2026-09-18 (local, America/Mazatlan).
- What this folder is: product screenshots published by an INDEPENDENT publisher, not
  by the vendor. The first pass could not reach the vendor screens, because every
  vendor keeps the back office behind a login. These files close that gap.
- Publisher: [The Restaurant HQ](https://www.therestauranthq.com/), a restaurant
  trade publisher. It is not owned by a vendor in the set.
- Device column: read from the image. A browser chrome, a status bar, or a tablet
  bezel decides it.
- Licence note: these are the publisher's images and the vendor's product screens.
  They stay in this research folder as evidence, with the source URL. Do not rehost
  them in a product or a marketing page.

## What these images add

1. **The floor device is real and it is a phone.** Partender shows a nine-item
   inventory AREA list on a phone. WISK shows one bottle and one count control on a
   phone. Crunchtime shows the count by storage location on a tablet. **Documented
   fact**, images above.
2. **The desk screens are dense tables in a desktop browser.** MarketMan, Backbar,
   Bar-i, and Lightspeed all publish a wide table with cost columns. None of them
   publishes a phone screen for the same task. **Documented fact**, images above.
3. **The name of the screen is the job, not the object.** "Which storage location",
   "which area", "count by case or unit". The screen asks the counting question.
   **Inference** from the images.

## Why the images in this folder are not in git

The screenshots are NOT committed. This index is the evidence: it holds the source
URL, the app id, the device class, and what each screen shows, so every image is
reproducible with the route recorded below.

The reason is size. The five harvest folders hold 193 images and 53 MB, against a
repository that already tracks 2.4 MB of research images and a 91 MB `.git`. The
`.gitignore` entry that keeps them out is documented in the repository root.

The curated evidence the reports cite IS committed: the eight official Lightspeed
screenshots at the root of this folder, and the before and after screen sets under
`docs/research/2026-09-18-design-taste/assets/screens/`.
